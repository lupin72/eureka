# Brief: Pantry new-user retention

## Problem

About 40% of Pantry's new users stop opening the app within three weeks of signing up, usually after their first or second planned week of dinners.

## Desired state

Most new users are still planning their week's dinners with Pantry two months after sign-up.

## Current situation

Pantry is a household meal-planning app: users plan the week's dinners and it builds the shopping list. It has 18,000 monthly active users, a library of 3,000 recipes, an email list, and a team of four: two engineers, one designer and one product lead.

## Constraints

- No new hires.
- Under €10,000 to spend over the next quarter.
- Changes must ship within three months.
- No selling or sharing user data.

## Out of bounds

- Discounts or free months.
- Push-notification campaigns (both tried last year).

## Obvious solution

Rework onboarding with a guided first-week setup and a tour of features, then send a lifecycle email sequence over the first eight weeks with reminders to plan the next week and personalised recipe suggestions.
