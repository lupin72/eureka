# Challenge ideas

## 1. Self-run swap board with shift captains

**Description:** The fixed weekly rota is replaced by a shared online board of single shifts that volunteers claim, drop and swap themselves, with a rotating volunteer "shift captain" each Saturday who checks the board is covered. People can give one Saturday a month, or three in a row and then none, and nobody has to explain an absence to the coordinator.
**Technique:** Challenge, assumption challenged: `a volunteer commits to a regular weekly slot managed by the coordinator`
**Movement:** Dropped the regular commitment and replaced the coordinator as scheduler with the volunteers themselves. Younger people avoid open-ended weekly obligations, and a self-service board fits their irregular lives while freeing the coordinator's four spare hours.
**Problem it solves:** It lowers the commitment barrier that keeps younger people away and stops burnout-driven leaving, so the active count can grow toward 40.
