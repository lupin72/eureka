# Concept Fan ideas

Fan:

- Purpose: enough reliable hands, from a wider spread of ages, to keep the warehouse and Saturday distribution running year after year
  - Direction: recruit more individual volunteers through publicity (the Obvious solution; not fanned further)
  - Direction: recruit groups instead of individuals
    - Concept: make an organisation, not a person, the volunteer
    - Concept: attach volunteering to something people already need
  - Direction: grow volunteers from inside the existing community
    - Concept: let current volunteers hand on their place
    - Concept: make the people who receive the hands that give
  - Direction: reshape the work to fit working-age lives
    - Concept: shrink the commitment unit
    - Concept: move the work to the hours younger people have free
    - Concept: fold the shift into something people already do for fun

## 1. Run to the Shelf

**Description:** Second Shelf partners with a local running club or parkrun group so that a Saturday social run finishes at the warehouse, where runners do a 90-minute distribution shift and share a breakfast paid from the budget. The run becomes the club's weekly ritual and the shift part of it.
**Technique:** Concept Fan, concept: `fold the shift into something people already do for fun`
**Movement:** Purpose → direction "reshape the work to fit working-age lives" → the concept attaches the shift to a leisure habit younger people already keep on Saturday mornings → a run route that ends at the food bank.
**Problem it solves:** It brings a steady weekly group of mostly under-50s to the Saturday distribution through a social habit rather than a one-off appeal.
