# Concept Fan ideas

Fan:

- Purpose: enough reliable hands, from a wider spread of ages, to keep the warehouse and Saturday distribution running year after year
  - Direction: recruit more individual volunteers through publicity (the Obvious solution; not fanned further)
  - Direction: recruit groups instead of individuals
    - Concept: make an organisation, not a person, the volunteer
    - Concept: attach volunteering to something people already need
  - Direction: grow volunteers from inside the existing community
    - Concept: let current volunteers hand on their place
    - Concept: make the people who receive the hands that give
  - Direction: reshape the work to fit working-age lives
    - Concept: shrink the commitment unit
    - Concept: move the work to the hours younger people have free
    - Concept: fold the shift into something people already do for fun

## 1. Adopt-a-Saturday

**Description:** Local employers, sports clubs and university societies each "adopt" one Saturday a month and send a team of five to eight people, who take the food-safety briefing together on a weekday visit. The coordinator manages a dozen organisational contacts instead of dozens of individual availabilities, and each team rotates its own members.
**Technique:** Concept Fan, concept: `make an organisation, not a person, the volunteer`
**Movement:** Purpose (reliable hands) → direction "recruit groups instead of individuals" → the concept moves the unit of commitment from a person to an organisation that refills its own roster → a monthly adopted Saturday per organisation.
**Problem it solves:** Each adopting team brings a renewable pool of mostly working-age people, so losing one individual no longer leaves a gap in the rota.
**Merged into:** ideas/random-entry.md › 7. Group lift bookings

## 2. The CV Shift

**Description:** Second Shelf markets volunteering to job-seekers, students and career-changers as a work-experience package: a food-safety briefing certificate, a logged record of hours in logistics and customer service, and a signed reference after three months. The coordinator writes references from a template, so it costs minutes, not hours.
**Technique:** Concept Fan, concept: `attach volunteering to something people already need`
**Movement:** Purpose → direction "recruit groups instead of individuals" → the concept looks for a need volunteers already have (a CV line, a reference) → the food-safety briefing, a constraint, becomes the hook, offered as a credential.
**Problem it solves:** It gives under-50s a self-interested reason to join and stay for months, which directly targets the missing younger third of the rota.
**Merged into:** ideas/random-entry.md › 3. Ski-school credentials

## 3. Shift Partner Succession

**Description:** Each over-65 regular is invited to bring one younger relative, neighbour or former colleague as a named "shift partner" who works alongside them and learns their role. When the older volunteer steps back, the partner already holds the slot and the know-how.
**Technique:** Concept Fan, concept: `let current volunteers hand on their place`
**Movement:** Purpose → direction "grow volunteers from inside the existing community" → the concept treats each departing volunteer as a succession event rather than a loss → a paired apprenticeship of a younger person with each older regular.
**Problem it solves:** It turns the 30 over-65 regulars, the source of the attrition, into the recruiters of their own younger replacements.
**Merged into:** ideas/random-entry.md › 5. Skis come in pairs

## 4. From Parcel to Crew

**Description:** Adults from recipient households are offered, with no pressure and no effect on their parcel, a place on the Saturday crew or the weekday warehouse team, with the briefing and a crew T-shirt. Many recipients are working-age and already come every Saturday, so they are among the most reliable people on site.
**Technique:** Concept Fan, concept: `make the people who receive the hands that give`
**Movement:** Purpose → direction "grow volunteers from inside the existing community" → the concept flips the 220 households from clients to a recruitment pool already present each Saturday → an opt-in crew invitation at the distribution line.
**Problem it solves:** It taps a large, younger, already-present group that the current recruitment never reaches, adding volunteers without any publicity spend.
**Merged into:** ideas/provocation.md › 1. Neighbour shifts for parcel households

## 5. The One-Hour Drop-In

**Description:** Alongside the core rota, Second Shelf opens a no-sign-up one-hour slot each Saturday morning, where anyone who has watched a ten-minute briefing video and passed a short quiz by QR code can turn up and help. Regular drop-ins are invited onto the rota after their fifth visit.
**Technique:** Concept Fan, concept: `shrink the commitment unit`
**Movement:** Purpose → direction "reshape the work to fit working-age lives" → the concept cuts the unit of commitment from a standing weekly shift to a single hour with no planning → a drop-in slot that works as a funnel into the rota.
**Problem it solves:** It lowers the entry barrier that keeps busy under-50s from committing, and converts the ones who return into stable rota members.
**Merged into:** ideas/provocation.md › 2. Turn up first, sign up later

## 6. After-Work Packing Nights

**Description:** Parcel packing moves from Saturday morning to two weekday evenings (for example Tuesday and Thursday, 18:00–20:00) in the warehouse, which is already open those days, so Saturday needs only a smaller distribution crew. Evening slots suit people with weekday jobs who keep Saturdays for family.
**Technique:** Concept Fan, concept: `move the work to the hours younger people have free`
**Movement:** Purpose → direction "reshape the work to fit working-age lives" → the concept splits the work by when it must happen: distribution is tied to Saturday, packing is not → packing shifts to weekday evenings when working adults are free.
**Problem it solves:** It creates volunteer slots that working-age people can actually fill, while distribution stays on Saturday as the constraint requires.
**Merged into:** ideas/provocation.md › 6. After-work warehouse evenings

## 7. Run to the Shelf

**Description:** Second Shelf partners with a local running club or parkrun group so that a Saturday social run finishes at the warehouse, where runners do a 90-minute distribution shift and share a breakfast paid from the budget. The run becomes the club's weekly ritual and the shift part of it.
**Technique:** Concept Fan, concept: `fold the shift into something people already do for fun`
**Movement:** Purpose → direction "reshape the work to fit working-age lives" → the concept attaches the shift to a leisure habit younger people already keep on Saturday mornings → a run route that ends at the food bank.
**Problem it solves:** It brings a steady weekly group of mostly under-50s to the Saturday distribution through a social habit rather than a one-off appeal.
