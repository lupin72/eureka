# Six Hats ideas

## 2. Regulars become shift captains

**Description:** Instead of losing over-65 volunteers when heavy shifts become too much, Second Shelf turns each of them into a seated "shift captain" who runs the briefing, the parcel checklist and the welcome for two or three newcomers. Each captain is responsible for bringing at least one person they know under 50 (a neighbour, a nephew, a former colleague) and mentoring them through their first month. The role is explicitly lighter on the body and heavier on knowledge, so the people most at risk of leaving get a reason to stay longer.
**Technique:** Six Hats, Green Hat seed: `The retiring volunteers are the recruitment channel, not the problem`
**Problem it solves:** It slows the outflow of older volunteers while using their networks to recruit younger ones, attacking both sides of the loss-versus-recruitment gap.
