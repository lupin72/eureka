# Brief: Second Shelf volunteer renewal

## Problem

Second Shelf, a volunteer-led food bank, is losing volunteers faster than it recruits them: 30 of its 45 regulars are over 65, and last year it lost eight and recruited two.

## Desired state

Within a year, a stable rota of at least 40 active volunteers, at least a third of them under 50.

## Current situation

Every Saturday Second Shelf hands out food parcels to about 220 households. It has a warehouse open Tuesday to Saturday, one part-time paid coordinator, a van, good relations with two supermarkets and a parish, and a Facebook page with 1,200 followers.

## Constraints

- Budget of €3,000 for the year.
- The coordinator has four spare hours a week.
- Volunteers must be over 18 and pass a short food-safety briefing.
- Distribution stays on Saturdays, because of the supermarket donation schedule.

## Out of bounds

- Paying volunteers.
- Partnering with political parties.

## Obvious solution

Run a recruitment drive: post volunteer calls on the Facebook page and in the parish newsletter, put up posters in the partner supermarkets, hold an open day at the warehouse, and thank volunteers with an annual appreciation event.
