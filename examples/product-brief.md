> We make Pantry, a meal-planning app for households: you plan the week's dinners and it builds the shopping list. About 40% of new users stop opening it within three weeks of signing up, usually after their first or second planned week. We want most new users still planning with Pantry two months after sign-up. Today we have 18,000 monthly active users, a library of 3,000 recipes, an email list, and a team of four (two engineers, one designer, one product lead). Constraints: no new hires, under €10,000 for the next quarter, changes must ship within three months, and no selling or sharing user data. Out of bounds: discounts or free months, and push-notification campaigns; we tried both last year.
