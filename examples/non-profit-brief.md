> We run Second Shelf, a volunteer-led food bank in a mid-sized town. Every Saturday we hand out food parcels to about 220 households, but our volunteers are ageing: 30 of our 45 regulars are over 65, and in the last year we lost eight and recruited two. We want a stable rota of at least 40 active volunteers, with at least a third under 50, within a year. Today we have a warehouse open Tuesday to Saturday, one part-time paid coordinator, a van, good relations with two supermarkets and a parish, and a Facebook page with 1,200 followers. Constraints: a budget of €3,000 for the year, the coordinator has four spare hours a week, volunteers must be over 18 and pass a short food-safety briefing, and distribution has to stay on Saturdays because of the supermarket donation schedule. Out of bounds: paying volunteers, and partnering with political parties.
